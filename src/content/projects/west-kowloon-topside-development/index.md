The site sits in West Kowloon, a district projected to become one of Hong Kong's emerging city centres, and was first conceived together with the adjacent XRL (Express Rail Link) station as part of a larger topside development plan. It lies on the continuum of the West Kowloon Parkway, the chain of urban green spaces running across the district to the waterfront. Planning approval issues suspended the original mixed-use scheme after the station and the site's own basement had already been built, so the new design had to build on what was already in the ground and make itself an integral part of the Parkway sequence.

KPF was invited by the owner, SHKP, into a competition to reconceive the site, and after being awarded the project continued with SHKP to develop the scheme for Town Planning Board submission. I designed the client-selected competition scheme and was the main contributor to the office towers in the submission phase, managing their massing and facade studies, plan studies, sectional stacking, GFA control and structural coordination. I also took part in the retail podium facade design.
